Privacy

LearnWise stores your word bank, reading history, and settings only on this device — there's no LearnWise server, no analytics, and no tracking. Reading history is saved by website domain only (full-URL logging is off by default). Nothing is sent anywhere unless you turn on smart translations, which use an AI provider and a key you supply.
