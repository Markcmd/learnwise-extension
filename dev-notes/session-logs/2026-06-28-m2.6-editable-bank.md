# 2026-06-28 — M2.6: editable word bank (M2 COMPLETE)

## Goal
Let users fix or remove words, and wipe everything — the last M2 task.

## What shipped
- **`core/wordbank.js`** (pure): `editWord` (meaning/pronunciation, trims +
  bumps `updatedAt`) and `deleteWord`.
- **`background.js`**: `MSG.DELETE_WORD` (record + its events) and
  `MSG.CLEAR_WORDBANK` (bank + sightings + IndexedDB events/reviews stores).
- **Settings:** per-word Edit (inline form) + Delete in the word detail; a
  danger card with Clear all words. Inlined `editWordIO` (direct) + delete/clear
  via background messages.
- **Tests:** `tests/wordbankEdit.test.js`. Manifest → 1.0.9.

## Decisions
- **Delete/clear go through the background; edit doesn't.** Deleting a word must
  also clear its IndexedDB events (otherwise derived familiarity would
  resurrect it on the next reading pass — same reason demote routes through the
  background). Editing only changes meaning/pronunciation in the bank
  (`chrome.storage.local`), which the settings page can write directly.
- **Clear-all keeps setup/settings.** It wipes the bank, sighting counters, and
  the event + review log, but preserves onboarding state, theme, and BYOK
  settings — "clear my words", not "factory reset".
- **Edit is deliberately limited to meaning + pronunciation.** Level/status are
  derived; letting users hand-edit them would fight the "store facts, derive
  scores" model.
- **`editWord` bumps `updatedAt`** so a manual fix wins the export/import merge
  (2.5) — your correction beats an older backup.
